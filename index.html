What Appelo does

Appelo reads the participant list on the Google Meet page during a call and notes when each person joined and left. When the call ends you get the attendance in one click, as a file that opens correctly in Excel (accents and Arabic included).

  • Live roster: who is in the call now, arrival time, minutes present and % of the meeting.
  • Rejoins handled: someone who drops and comes back is counted once, with the right total time.
  • Export to CSV (free) or Excel (Pro).
  • Pro: class lists to find who was absent, late or only partly present; history of all meetings with search; attendance report over time per class; automatic export when the call ends.
  • Six languages: English, Italian, French, Spanish, German and Arabic (right-to-left).

Your data stays with you

There is no Appelo server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Attendance, class lists and settings are saved only in your own browser. The only network request Appelo ever makes is the license check for Pro users. Read the privacy policy (six languages).
